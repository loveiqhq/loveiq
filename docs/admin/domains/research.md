# Research, Strategy, and Intelligence

Use this domain for research workspaces, experiments, benchmarks, metric systems, predictions, strategy planning, and higher-order intelligence layers.

## What belongs here

- Research repository, taxonomy, and synthesis workspaces
- Experiments, benchmarks, predictions, and strategy views
- Metric registry, metric status, metric lineage, and intelligence builders

## What does not belong here

- Shell navigation and operating controls: use [`command-center.md`](command-center.md)
- Growth and funnel analytics: use [`growth.md`](growth.md)
- Health diagnostics and review queues: use [`health.md`](health.md)
