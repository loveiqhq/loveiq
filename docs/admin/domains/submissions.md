# Submissions and Moderation

Use this domain for submission review, notes, detail drilldowns, exports, saved views, and survey-status controls.

## What belongs here

- Submission list, detail, compare, and partial-session recovery surfaces
- Notes, comments, tagging, bulk actions, exports, and moderation controls
- Survey availability toggles used by operators

## What does not belong here

- Shell-wide navigation and leadership dashboards: use [`command-center.md`](command-center.md)
- Scoring, archetype, and report analytics: use [`scoring.md`](scoring.md)
