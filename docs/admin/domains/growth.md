# Growth, Funnels, Journey, and Revenue

Use this domain for conversion analysis, growth loops, funnel health, journey mapping, replay, retention, pulse, and revenue reporting.

## What belongs here

- Funnel, comparison, and growth-control dashboards
- Invite network, journey, replay, retention, pulse, and pipeline flows
- Revenue and transaction analytics

## What does not belong here

- Submission moderation: use [`submissions.md`](submissions.md)
- Product KPI and archetype analysis: use [`scoring.md`](scoring.md)
- Operational health and audit tooling: use [`health.md`](health.md)
