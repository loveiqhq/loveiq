# Scoring and Survey Intelligence

Use this domain for survey analytics, archetypes, product KPIs, reporting, text analysis, profiles, and the standalone stats dashboard.

## What belongs here

- Scoring, scorecard, risk, archetype, and profile surfaces
- Product KPI, question-lifecycle, question-effectiveness, and report views
- Text analysis, language analytics, answer distributions, and search over survey content
- The dedicated stats-heavy dashboard component backed by `/api/admin/stats`

## What does not belong here

- Growth and retention funnels: use [`growth.md`](growth.md)
- Research repository and strategy planning: use [`research.md`](research.md)
- Submission moderation flows: use [`submissions.md`](submissions.md)
