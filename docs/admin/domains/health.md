# Health and Operational Diagnostics

Use this domain for system health, incident analysis, alerting, review queues, audits, release impact, and operational risk surfaces.

## What belongs here

- Health dashboards, anomaly and drift diagnostics, incident views
- Alerts, reviews, annotations, audit feeds, and workspace maturity tooling
- Release impact, tech intelligence, and operational risk views

## What does not belong here

- Strategy and research modeling: use [`research.md`](research.md)
- Core shell navigation and goals: use [`command-center.md`](command-center.md)
- Pure revenue, funnel, or scoring analysis: use [`growth.md`](growth.md) or [`scoring.md`](scoring.md)
