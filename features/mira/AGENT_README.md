# features/mira

**Purpose:** Mira, the AI coach for body confidence in sex. Beta MVP agreed in the AI Mira Synch (17 Nov 2026), built from Marcus's canvas "AI coach chat" (page "MVP Scope", the green row) and Sanjin's conversation design.

**Entry:**

- `app/mira/page.tsx` → `ui/MiraChat.tsx`: boards 01 (anonymous chat), 01b (free limit), 03 (coaching style) and 08a (chats, signed in), and the "Keep this chat" card from board "01 Anonymous chat (embed) copy". A stored chat's link (`/mira?chat=`, a nudge's button) opened while signed out goes to `/mira/start?next=…` and back to that chat after sign-in.
- `app/mira/start` → `ui/SignUpFlow.tsx`: boards 04, 05, 06 (email code; no WhatsApp step). After sign-in (code or Google) it goes on to `next` when `server/session.ts` `safeNext` allows it (only `/mira`, `/mira?chat=<id>`, `/mira/home`, `/mira/settings`, `/mira/feedback`, spelt exactly), else Home.
- `app/mira/home` → `ui/MiraHome.tsx`: boards 08 and 08b. `app/mira/settings` → `ui/MiraSettings.tsx`: board 08c, cut for the beta. `app/mira/feedback` → `ui/MiraFeedback.tsx`: board 10. `app/mira/privacy`: Mira's privacy notice. `app/mira/guide`: the body confidence guide, open to everyone (text in `content/guide.ts`); Home's Guides tile links to it (`NEXT_PUBLIC_MIRA_GUIDE_URL` overrides).
- API: `app/api/mira/chat`, `auth`, `me`, `chats/[id]`, `event` (see `docs/api.md`).
- Tables: `mira_user`, `mira_chat`, `mira_message`, `mira_event` (`supabase/migrations/20261118120000_mira_mvp.sql`).

**What Mira reads (`content/`):** `system-prompt.ts` (Sanjin's rules, voice, safety rails, compiled 18 Nov), `mira-config.json` (opening message, chips, intake questions, coaching-style scales, the fixed crisis, under-18 and refusal messages), `evidence-cards.json` (the only research she may use), `guide.ts` (the guide; its practices' steps are added to her cached instructions by `server/prompt.ts`, so she gives them as the guide writes them), `crisis-lines.json` (Sanjin's 9 countries, every line checked on its operator's site on 20 Nov 2026; the support panel shows who each line is for, its hours, and text-only lines as text links with their keyword). The prompt and the cards are cached by the model provider; the person's own context (style, name, gender, country, LoveIQ result) is added per message and never logged.

**Calls taken for the beta (Eman delegated them, 18 Nov 2026):**

- Anyone can open the chat, at `/mira` on loveiq.org, branded LoveIQ: "Mira, the AI coach by LoveIQ" (team, 18 Nov 2026), with the site's LoveIQ logo, page titles, the sign-in code's sender name and the prompt's identity line. The legal entity, Applied Psychometrics UG (haftungsbeschränkt), appears only in legal texts and footers; nudge emails keep the neutral sender "Mira".
- Free limit: an anonymous browser may send 10 messages (`ANON_FREE_MESSAGES`, counted per browser id over 30 days; the team moved it from Marcus's "after 10 conversations" on 20 Nov 2026). The reply to the 10th brings the sign-up card (not beside a crisis reply) and the 11th needs the free account. Messages are capped at 50 a day per person (`DAILY_MESSAGES`; 300 a day per network for anonymous chats). A crisis message is never counted or stopped by a limit.
- "Keep this chat" (board 01 copy): after Mira's first reply to an anonymous person, a card asks whether Mira should remember the chat; "Keep this chat" goes to sign-up (the chat comes along), "Not now" puts it away for that chat (kept with the chat in this browser). Never beside the intake invitation (it follows the next reply instead), the NPS question or a crisis in that chat, never to a signed-in person. Logged as `keep_card_shown`, `keep_card_keep`, `keep_card_not_now`.
- Consent (version `mira-2026-11-21`, `CONSENT_VERSION`; a browser that ticked an older one is asked again). Chat: one required tick (18+, sex life and health, analytics see what is typed) and an optional "Mira's team may read replies I rate" that starts empty, both logged as `consent_given` with the version (`shareRated`). Sign-up: 18+, the terms of use and privacy notice, and the sex life and health processing are required; "Use my LoveIQ test result" (`mira_user.use_assessment`, off by default since migration 20261121020000) and the rating box (`share_rated_replies`) are optional and start empty, and every sign-in records the form as ticked that time. A rated reply's words are kept in `mira_event` only for an anonymous person who ticked the rating box; otherwise the thumbs only (a signed-in rating lives on their own stored message).
- The sign-up email form has a honeypot: a `website` field people never see or reach (aria-hidden, out of the tab order), sent with the code request so the auth route can drop the bots that fill it.
- Mira's privacy notice lives here; Eman checked it on 20 Nov 2026.
- Model: Claude Sonnet, with Claude Haiku when it is down (Sanjin's coach config, board 09, RFD 18 Nov): `MIRA_LLM_KEY`, `MIRA_LLM_MODEL`, `MIRA_LLM_FALLBACK_MODEL`. The board asked for EU-only processing, which Anthropic's own API doesn't offer; Eman decided on 19 Nov to run on Anthropic's API (the privacy page says messages may be processed outside the EU).
- Sign-in by emailed code or with Google (`server/google.ts`, our own OAuth code flow with PKCE, scope `openid email`, trusted only for Gmail or Workspace addresses (`hd`) and never for `admin_users`, then the same Supabase user as the code; `MIRA_GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` + `MIRA_GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`, from the "Mira web" client in the Google Cloud project `loveiq-mira`, consent screen "Mira", External, In production; the console never shows the secret again, a copy is in Eman's Keychain). No Apple yet (it needs a paid Apple Developer account). Typing only (no spoken Talk), no session reminders yet, daily nudge emails built but off until `MIRA_NUDGES_ENABLED` is on (below), no admin screens (change `content/` instead).

**Safety:** a message plainly about suicide or self-harm gets Sanjin's message E and the support lines without a model call (`server/safety.ts`: narrow patterns for English slang and typos and for German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Turkish, Russian, Arabic and Bosnian/Croatian/Serbian, each with tests for the idioms it must leave alone); the model ends a reply with `[[CRISIS]]` when a stop rail fires, which opens the panel. A reply the model declines or leaves empty gets a neutral line (`refusalMessage`) and opens the panel too, logged as `reply_declined` with the refusal category, never as `crisis_shown`. Under 18 (rail 15, 21 Nov): the model ends its reply with `[[MINOR]]`, the person gets message G (`minorMessage`) and, from then on, every message from that browser (`mira_minor` cookie, a year) or account (`mira_user.minor_at`) gets G without the model, except a crisis message, which still gets E and the lines first (`server/minor.ts`, event `minor_stop`). The account's nudges and "Remember our conversations" go off, its chats are deleted, and the nudge job skips it; the browser forgets its chat and its 18+ tick. A minor stop without signs of danger no longer opens the panel (Sanjin: no youth helplines). Rules never depend on the coaching style.

**Accounts:** Supabase Auth in the same project as the admin panel and Jarvis, under Mira's own cookie (`mira-auth`). A code is checked only if Mira emailed it to that address within the hour, with 25 guesses per code reserved atomically before the check (`mira_sign_in_code`, `mira_claim_code_attempt`) and 5 per 10 minutes per address and network, so the route can't be used to guess anyone else's code (25 tries against 100 million codes) and one stranger can't spend a person's guesses. Supabase's own public verify endpoint is limited by Supabase (30 per 5 minutes per IP); codes live an hour. Deleting a Mira account keeps the shared login of staff and Jarvis members (only `mira_user` goes). Safe because nothing trusts "any signed-in user": admin routes check `admin_users`, Jarvis checks its members, no table grants the `authenticated` role anything.

**Sanjin's final coach config (board 09, Sanjin review; Marcus: "RFD for MVP", 18 Nov):** the greeting, the intake invitation with its two reply buttons (sent by the chat route after Mira's first reply, stored as its own message), 12 intake questions (age as a range first; gender comes from sign-up), the do and don't rules, the stop / refer / continue / hand-over table (Jonas for libido and Ana for asking a partner are not live: Mira says they are coming soon), the legal scope, the lessons from his five example chats, replies of at most 250 words, Sonnet with Haiku as fallback. The greeting is Sanjin's word for word (Eman's call, 18 Nov). Mira never promises a calendar invite (none can be sent yet).

**Daily nudges and memory (built 18 Nov, Eman: "build it"):** `server/nudges.ts` (who is due, which kinds fit, the model's body, the email), `server/nudge-prefs.ts`, `content/nudges.json` (Sanjin's six kinds and instructions), `app/api/cron/mira-nudges` (every 30 min, behind `MIRA_NUDGES_ENABLED`), `app/mira/nudges` + `app/api/mira/nudges/*` (Marcus's unsubscribe boards 2 to 4b, signed links, one-click unsubscribe), `app/api/mira/nudge` (the button: counts the click, opens the chat where the nudge is Mira's latest message). Settings: on or off, 8:00 / 19:00 / 21:30, time zone (from the browser at sign-up). Each person is due for the 60 minutes from their time, so every slot gets two of the half-hourly runs; the second sends to anyone the first did not reach or whose email failed (a failed email gives the day back and never reaches the chat). A run nudges 4 people at a time and starts nobody after 225 s of its 300 s, and a run that stopped early or had failures is recorded as an error. At most one a day, never the same kind twice in a row (counting only nudges that went), progress at most once a week (the first a week after sign-up), none while chatting (12 h) or for 14 days after a crisis, none to suppressed addresses, none without remembered chats. Subjects are neutral, the kind's teaser only with no suffix, and a hidden neutral line ("A short note for you.") is what an inbox preview shows (Eman, 20 Nov 2026, because the sign-up page promises a neutral sender name and subject line). `app/api/cron/mira-memory` deletes a person's chats 12 months after the last message they wrote (`mira_forget_old_memory`), and sign-in codes issued more than a day ago.

**Outcomes for the MVP success board (20 Nov 2026):** the model ends a reply with markers the app reads and removes (`server/safety.ts` `parseReply`; asked for in `server/prompt.ts`, "MARKERS FOR THE APP"): `[[ASK:id]]` when it asks an intake question, whose answers the chat then shows as buttons (`ui/IntakeAnswers.tsx`; the id is stored as `mira_message.ask`), `[[REFER]]` when it suggests a therapist or doctor, and `[[TOPIC:id]]` on a new chat's first reply in the person's own words (`mira-config.json` `topics`, Marcus's board). The chat route logs them as `mira_event` rows by id, never words: `intake_answered` (the browser sends `answering` when Mira's last reply asked a question, tapped or typed; a skip sends none), `referred`, `chat_topic`. The body check (`server/body-check.ts`, `ui/BodyCheck.tsx`, `app/api/mira/body-check`) is the Body Appreciation Scale-2 (Tylka & Wood-Barcalow 2015; free to use, items unchanged, word for word in `mira-config.json` `bodyCheck`), offered in the chat to signed-in people when due: once at the start, once more from 28 days later; "Not now" waits three days in that browser. AP › Mira (beta) counts an improvement or a worsening at 0.8 points or more (the reliable change index from the paper's SD and test–retest reliability) and profile depth over five areas: root-cause screen (answered `where` and `onset`), baseline (a first body check), goals (answered `change`), preferences (a coaching style), other LoveIQ assessments.
