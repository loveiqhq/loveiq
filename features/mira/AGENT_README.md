**Safety:** a message plainly about suicide or self-harm gets Sanjin's message E and the support lines without a model call (`server/safety.ts`: narrow patterns for English slang and typos and for German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Turkish, Russian, Arabic and Bosnian/Croatian/Serbian, each with tests for the idioms it must leave alone); the model ends a reply with `[[CRISIS]]` when a stop rail fires, which opens the panel. The account's nudges and "Remember our conversations" go off, its chats and everything else it keeps are deleted (goals, journal, self-check and body check results, voice sessions, rating, passkeys, weekly session, Notify me requests and the finder's outcome, a pending change of email, and from round 2 its beta feedback with any address left for a follow-up and the note on a nudge stop, as account deletion removes them: `server/minor.ts`, each step on its own so one failure never stops the rest), and the nudge job skips it; the browser forgets its chat and its 18+ tick. The chat's "Need support right now?" lists a signed-in person's "Where you're based" first, or "Somewhere else" for a country not listed (round-2 audit SET-9; the frame's link is the frame's).

**Accounts:** Supabase Auth in the same project as the admin panel and Jarvis, under Mira's own cookie (`mira-auth`). A code is checked only if Mira emailed it to that address within the hour, with 25 guesses per code reserved atomically before the check (`mira_sign_in_code`, `mira_claim_code_attempt`) and 5 per 10 minutes per address and network, so the route can't be used to guess anyone else's code (25 tries against 100 million codes) and one stranger can't spend a person's guesses. Supabase's own public verify endpoint is limited by Supabase (30 per 5 minutes per IP); codes live an hour. Code emails go through the Resend account that sends all LoveIQ mail: one per inbox per 10 minutes and five a day, ten an hour per network, sixty an hour in all (which alerts the team); never to a suppressed address (`email_suppression`) or when that can't be checked, nor when the hidden `website` field is filled, each answered exactly as a sent code. Logins Mira's sign-in creates are marked (`app_metadata.created_by` "mira"); the daily memory cron deletes those over a day old with no `mira_user` row and no code from the last day, through `deleteAccount`. Deleting a Mira account keeps the shared login of staff and Jarvis members (only `mira_user` goes). Safe because nothing trusts "any signed-in user": admin routes check `admin_users`, Jarvis checks its members, no table grants the `authenticated` role anything.

**Weekly sessions (21 Nov 2026; Marcus: "a weekly 15-min session question with time slots"; Sanjin: "Always schedule the next session and send a calendar invite"):**

- One weekly slot per account, 15 minutes (`mira_session_slot`: ISO weekday, local time, IANA zone, `sequence`, `paused_at`; reminders in `mira_session_reminder`; migration `20261122050000_mira_sessions.sql`). `server/calendar.ts` is pure: the next start, found day by day on the zone's own calendar (a time in the spring gap is read with the offset before it, one in the autumn overlap is the first, as RFC 5545 says), the hand-written .ics (CRLF lines folded at 75 octets, escaping, a weekly repeat rule, and the time zone's definition, derived from ICU and checked against 11 years, left out when no simple yearly rule fits, as for Israel) and the Google Calendar link. `server/weekly-sessions.ts` stores it (a move keeps the id, the invite's UID, and raises the sequence; a cancel deletes the row, so a new booking is a new series); `server/session-emails.ts` sends the invite, its changes and the cancellation (.ics REQUEST or CANCEL attached, from "Mira", neutral words, never chat content) and runs the reminders.
- Booking: the weekly question (`[[ASK:weekly]]`, answer type `slots`) shows `ui/weekly-session/SlotPicker.tsx` `ChatSlotPicker` under Mira's question (weekday chips, Sanjin's times, "Another time"); signed out, an invitation to the free account. Booking saves and emails first, then the person's answer goes into the chat. Home's `NextSessionCard` and Settings' `WeeklySessionSettings` (in Settings' anchor `#weekly`, where the session emails link) open `ScheduleSheet`. API: `/api/mira/weekly-session` (GET, `?format=ics`, PUT, PATCH, DELETE).
- What Mira knows: every signed-in message carries a WEEKLY SESSION section (`prompt.ts` `weeklySessionInstructions`: booked or not, nothing when it can't be read), so she offers the picker and says a session is booked only when it is. A message that answers her weekly question while a slot exists was booked with the picker a moment ago, and the section says so, so she doesn't say sorry that it "was already booked" (round-2 audit CHAT-13).
- The session: the invite and the reminder link to `/mira/session` (sign-in, no token), a new chat in which the app sends "I’m here for my weekly session." and Mira opens the session, picking up from the last chat she remembers (only with "Remember our conversations" on; never opening with the content of a chat that held a crisis in the last 14 days; no topic marker or intake invitation). One chat per session (`sessionKey`: the start from half an hour back, or the UTC day with nothing booked): a later visit (a reload, Back, the link again) reopens it (`sessionChat`), and the browser remembers the key so it never sends a second opening (`mira.session.v1`); next week's session is a new chat. A session is open from its reminder (an hour before the start) to 30 minutes after it: Home shows "Join now", and a visit to the session page then is attendance for a reminded start (`joined_at`, once per start).
- Reminders: `app/api/cron/mira-session-reminders`, every 15 minutes (2,17,32,47), 30 to 60 minutes before each start, so each start's window has two runs and gets one email (claimed before sending; a failed send gives the claim back). Production host only; never to paused slots, an address that bounced or complained (an unsubscribe from LoveIQ's other emails doesn't stop what the person booked), or an account stopped as under 18, nor for 14 days after a crisis, as the nudges. Stopping Mira's nudges from an email (the page or one-click) pauses them too.
- Emails (22 Nov 2026): every Mira email (session emails, nudges, the journal reminder) is built by `server/email-shell.ts` `miraEmail`: a white card on a soft grey page, Mira's portrait (`public/images/mira/mira-email.jpg`, a JPG because Outlook can't show WebP) and name, a Lora heading, an ink pill button (full width on phones), and a light footer with the links, the reason for the email and the company line (`COMPANY_LINE`). A session email shows its time as a calendar entry: the next start's date tile, "Tuesdays at 19:00", the zone and 15 minutes, read in the session's own zone; a cancelled time is struck through. Dark mode and phone spacing come from a `<style>` block that clients without it can ignore; fonts load from the site (`/fonts/`), with Georgia and the system sans as fallbacks. Words stay neutral (a shared inbox), and the HTML and text parts come from the same words.
- Stops and links: each session email has a one-click stop (RFC 8058) and a stop link signed for that purpose only (`session-stop`: it pauses reminders and nothing else; nudge tokens don't work there, nor it on nudge links); the session, Settings and Home links need the sign-in. Under 18: `stopForMinor` deletes the slot without any email, and the session routes answer 403 with `minor: true` (the pages hide sessions only for that 403; any other refusal is shown, to retry). Session emails share one cap, 10 a day per account. A cancellation that can't be emailed comes back from DELETE as its calendar update (`ics`), which Settings offers to download; deleting the account, or changing its email, sends a cancellation to the address that goes (best effort, within the cap).
- KPI: `session_scheduled`, `session_changed`, `session_paused`, `session_resumed`, `session_cancelled`, `session_reminder_sent`, `session_attended` (minutes from the start, a number); AP › Mira › Engagement & retention › "Weekly sessions": booked, in place, reminders sent, joined on time, cancelled.
- Known limit: an entry added with the Google Calendar link is the person's own copy, so a change or cancel can't reach it (the "new time" and cancellation emails say so); the attached invite does update. When the invite couldn't be emailed, the chat's picker shows the calendar links before the answer goes into the chat, and Mira is never told the invite is in their inbox.

**Alerts to the owner (20 Nov 2026, `server/alerts.ts`):** `alertTeam` emails `MIRA_ALERT_EMAIL` (default `ec@loveiq.org`) in plain text from `RESEND_FROM` and posts to the ops channel, at most once per key a day (counted in Upstash; per server instance without it, and sent anyway when it errors). It fires when Anthropic says the AI budget is used up (`model.ts` `isBudgetExhausted`: a spend limit we set, the tier's monthly cap, no credit, a billing problem; the fallback model is not tried and the chat answers "Mira is resting"), when this month's estimated AI spend crosses 80% and 100% of `MIRA_MONTHLY_BUDGET_USD` (default 500, kept equal to the Claude Console workspace limit, which is what stops her; every reply's token use, chat and nudges, is priced with `MODEL_PRICES` and added up per UTC month in Upstash), when the 800-a-day ceiling for everyone is reached (counted per UTC day, so resting ends at midnight UTC), and when the hourly cap on sign-in code emails is hit.

**Data-subject requests:** the admin tool (`features/admin/server/data-subject.ts`) exports a person's `mira_user`, chats and messages, body checks, self-checks (`mira_check`), goals and their ticks, the journal, voice sessions, the rating and its words, passkeys (ids, labels, public keys), devices, a pending change of email, the weekly session and its reminders, nudges, KPI events and a pending sign-in code, and erases them through `deleteAccount`.
