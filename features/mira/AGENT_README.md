# features/mira

**Purpose:** Mira, the AI coach for body confidence in sex. Beta MVP agreed in the AI Mira Synch (17 Nov 2026), built from Marcus's canvas "AI coach chat" (page "MVP Scope", the green row) and Sanjin's conversation design.

**Entry:**

**What Mira reads (`content/`):** `system-prompt.ts` (Sanjin's rules, voice, safety rails, compiled 18 Nov), `mira-config.json` (opening message, not shown since the minimal chat of 21 Nov 2026, chips, intake questions, coaching-style scales, the fixed crisis, under-18 and refusal messages), `evidence-cards.json` (the only research she may use), `guide.ts` (the guide; its practices' steps are added to her cached instructions by `server/prompt.ts`, so she gives them as the guide writes them), `crisis-lines.json` (Sanjin's 9 countries, every line checked on its operator's site on 20 Nov 2026; the support panel shows who each line is for, its hours, and text-only lines as text links with their keyword). The prompt and the cards are cached by the model provider; the person's own context (style, name, gender, country, LoveIQ result) is added per message and never logged.

**Safety:** a message plainly about suicide or self-harm gets Sanjin's message E and the support lines without a model call (`server/safety.ts`: narrow patterns for English slang and typos and for German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Turkish, Russian, Arabic and Bosnian/Croatian/Serbian, each with tests for the idioms it must leave alone); the model ends a reply with `[[CRISIS]]` when a stop rail fires, which opens the panel. A reply the model declines or leaves empty gets a neutral line (`refusalMessage`) and opens the panel too, logged as `reply_declined` with the refusal category, never as `crisis_shown`. Under 18 (rail 15, 21 Nov): the model ends its reply with `[[MINOR]]`, the person gets message G (`minorMessage`) and, from then on, every message from that browser (`mira_minor` cookie, a year) or account (`mira_user.minor_at`) gets G without the model, except a crisis message, which still gets E and the lines first (`server/minor.ts`, event `minor_stop`). The account's nudges and "Remember our conversations" go off, its chats are deleted, and the nudge job skips it; the browser forgets its chat and its 18+ tick. A minor stop without signs of danger no longer opens the panel (Sanjin: no youth helplines). Rules never depend on the coaching style.

**Accounts:** Supabase Auth in the same project as the admin panel and Jarvis, under Mira's own cookie (`mira-auth`). A code is checked only if Mira emailed it to that address within the hour, with 25 guesses per code reserved atomically before the check (`mira_sign_in_code`, `mira_claim_code_attempt`) and 5 per 10 minutes per address and network, so the route can't be used to guess anyone else's code (25 tries against 100 million codes) and one stranger can't spend a person's guesses. Supabase's own public verify endpoint is limited by Supabase (30 per 5 minutes per IP); codes live an hour. Code emails go through the Resend account that sends all LoveIQ mail: one per inbox per 10 minutes and five a day, ten an hour per network, sixty an hour in all (which alerts the team); never to a suppressed address (`email_suppression`) or when that can't be checked, nor when the hidden `website` field is filled, each answered exactly as a sent code. Logins Mira's sign-in creates are marked (`app_metadata.created_by` "mira"); the daily memory cron deletes those over a day old with no `mira_user` row and no code from the last day, through `deleteAccount`. Deleting a Mira account keeps the shared login of staff and Jarvis members (only `mira_user` goes). Safe because nothing trusts "any signed-in user": admin routes check `admin_users`, Jarvis checks its members, no table grants the `authenticated` role anything.

**Daily nudges and memory (built 18 Nov, Eman: "build it"):** `server/nudges.ts` (who is due, which kinds fit, the model's body, the email), `server/nudge-prefs.ts`, `content/nudges.json` (Sanjin's six kinds and instructions), `app/api/cron/mira-nudges` (every 30 min, behind `MIRA_NUDGES_ENABLED`), `app/mira/nudges` + `app/api/mira/nudges/*` (Marcus's unsubscribe boards 2 to 4b, signed links, one-click unsubscribe), `app/api/mira/nudge` (the button: counts the click, opens the chat where the nudge is Mira's latest message). Settings: on or off, 8:00 / 19:00 / 21:30, time zone (from the browser at sign-up). Each person is due for the 60 minutes from their time, so every slot gets two of the half-hourly runs; the second sends to anyone the first did not reach or whose email failed (a failed email gives the day back and never reaches the chat). A run nudges 4 people at a time and starts nobody after 225 s of its 300 s, and a run that stopped early or had failures is recorded as an error. At most one a day, never the same kind twice in a row (counting only nudges that went), progress at most once a week (the first a week after sign-up), none while chatting (12 h) or for 14 days after a crisis, none to suppressed addresses, none without remembered chats. Subjects are neutral, the kind's teaser only with no suffix, and a hidden neutral line ("A short note for you.") is what an inbox preview shows (Eman, 20 Nov 2026, because the sign-up page promises a neutral sender name and subject line). `app/api/cron/mira-memory` deletes a person's chats 12 months after the last message they wrote (`mira_forget_old_memory`), sign-in codes issued more than a day ago, `mira_event` rows older than 12 months (an id range at a time from the oldest) and unfinished sign-ups (see Accounts), starting nothing new after 20 s of its 60 s.

**Alerts to the owner (20 Nov 2026, `server/alerts.ts`):** `alertTeam` emails `MIRA_ALERT_EMAIL` (default `ec@loveiq.org`) in plain text from `RESEND_FROM` and posts to the ops channel, at most once per key a day (counted in Upstash; per server instance without it, and sent anyway when it errors). It fires when Anthropic says the AI budget is used up (`model.ts` `isBudgetExhausted`: a spend limit we set, the tier's monthly cap, no credit, a billing problem; the fallback model is not tried and the chat answers "Mira is resting"), when this month's estimated AI spend crosses 80% and 100% of `MIRA_MONTHLY_BUDGET_USD` (default 500, kept equal to the Claude Console workspace limit, which is what stops her; every reply's token use, chat and nudges, is priced with `MODEL_PRICES` and added up per UTC month in Upstash), when the 800-a-day ceiling for everyone is reached (counted per UTC day, so resting ends at midnight UTC), and when the hourly cap on sign-in code emails is hit.

**Data-subject requests:** the admin tool (`features/admin/server/data-subject.ts`) exports a person's `mira_user`, chats and messages, body checks, nudges, KPI events and a pending sign-in code, and erases them through `deleteAccount`.

**Outcomes for the MVP success board (20 Nov 2026):** the model ends a reply with markers the app reads and removes (`server/safety.ts` `parseReply`; asked for in `server/prompt.ts`, "MARKERS FOR THE APP"): `[[ASK:id]]` when it asks an intake question, whose answers the chat then shows as buttons (`ui/IntakeAnswers.tsx`; the id is stored as `mira_message.ask`), `[[REFER]]` when it suggests a therapist or doctor, and `[[TOPIC:id]]` on a new chat's first reply in the person's own words (`mira-config.json` `topics`, Marcus's board). The chat route logs them as `mira_event` rows by id, never words: `intake_answered` (the browser sends `answering` when Mira's last reply asked a question, tapped or typed; a skip sends none), `referred`, `chat_topic`. The body check (`server/body-check.ts`, `ui/BodyCheck.tsx`, `app/api/mira/body-check`) is the Body Appreciation Scale-2 (Tylka & Wood-Barcalow 2015; free to use, items unchanged, word for word in `mira-config.json` `bodyCheck`), offered in the chat to signed-in people when due: once at the start, once more from 28 days later; "Not now" waits three days in that browser. AP › Mira (beta) counts an improvement or a worsening at 0.8 points or more (the reliable change index from the paper's SD and test–retest reliability) and profile depth over five areas: root-cause screen (answered `where` and `onset`), baseline (a first body check), goals (answered `change`), preferences (a coaching style), other LoveIQ assessments.
