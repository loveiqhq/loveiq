**A/B mechanics:** `proxy.ts` `resolveLandingVariant()` sends bots to `white_video` (the video page, but no arm), honours `?variant=`, keeps a returning visitor's live arm from the sticky `__liq_lv` cookie (an earlier round's value is re-rolled), and otherwise flips a coin; `app/page.tsx` reads the `x-landing-variant` request header and renders the page — `pain_point` and `pain_point_c` → `LandingPagePainPoint` (whose `PHero` takes the version), `white_prev` → `LandingPageWhiteV1`, anything else → `LandingPageWhite`, which gives the hero the video for `video` and `white_video`. `shared/experiments/landingVariant.ts` owns the types, the live arms, the experiment id, round 3's dates (`LANDING_HERO_RESTART_DAY`, `LANDING_HERO_LAST_DAY`), B's days (`LANDING_PAIN_POINT_START_DAY`, `LANDING_PAIN_POINT_LAST_DAY`), the day the current comparison's readouts start (`LANDING_TEST_START_DAY`) and the downstream attribution notes. `white-v1/` pins only the four sections the rebuild redesigned (hero, archetype carousel, FAQ, closing CTA) and imports the other eleven from `white/`.

**Hero video (the video page, round 4's arm A; round 3's arm B):** `WHeroVideo` waits on the poster, paused, with a play button low in the middle (on Emma's chest, so her face stays clear): the old corner pill's circle, the brand gradient with a white triangle, 52px, 72px from `sm`, with a small pulse (Tailwind's `animate-play-pulse`; none under reduced motion or while the film starts); on a mouse's hover it grows a little and the poster dims. The running time sits bottom right. It plays the full video (`public/videos/white/`, 1:10, captions burned in) with sound on a tap; a start that fails (a rejection, a media error, or no frame within 12 s) brings the button back. Nothing plays by itself: the muted 7.5-second loop added at the 06.10 sync was removed at the 29.10 one (Marcus: it might be why people did not tap). It is imported statically, not with `next/dynamic`: that wrapper has no Suspense boundary, so arm B's whole page waited on the video's chunk to hydrate. Its tap, play, progress, completion and error events are in `features/analytics/client`.

**What the page records (both arms, PostHog and GA4 on every visit, GA4 cookieless without analytics consent):** `landing_page_view` and `experiment_exposure` (`LandingPageTracker`); `cta_click` from every call to action, by `location`, with `element: "question_card"` when it is a card's Continue rather than the button beside it; `cta_seen` once per page for `hero_card`, `hero_video` (half in view) and `sticky_bar` (when it slides in); `landing_card_answer` for a tap on question 1's scale on a card (`location`, `changed`, and `value` 1-7 for PostHog only, never GA4: special-category data kept in PostHog by owner decision, DPIA §6; with it, since 2026-11-24, `question`, the survey question the card asks: `10003` on version C, `01002` everywhere else); `section_seen` once per section per page when part of it reaches the top half of the screen or all of it is on screen (`LandingSectionsSeen`, `data-landing-section` on each section's root, the hero aside); `section_time` with `section` and `visible_ms`, the time a section was in view (at least half of it on screen, or across the middle of the screen) while the tab showed, only the part not sent before, as a beacon when the tab hides, on `pagehide` and on unmount (same component; the hero is timed too, through `data-landing-time="hero"` on `WHero`, but has no `section_seen` because every view starts there); `faq_expanded`; the video's events: `hero_video_tap` at the tap (`replay`; the click-through, since 29.10), `hero_video_play` once frames show (`replay`), `hero_video_progress` (`percent` 25/50/75, `replay`), `hero_video_complete` (`replay`), `hero_video_paused` and `_resumed`, and `hero_video_error` for a start that failed (`reason`), `abandoned` by beacon when the visitor left while it started. AP-Analytics → Landing page → Hero video counts them per person against `cta_seen hero_video`. Every other click reaches PostHog through autocapture. `features/landing/tests/landingTracking.test.tsx` pins the new ones.

**The new page's v2 (23.11, Mark's canvas comments and Hana's board "animated pain-point section"):** the six pain points are a carousel (`PPainPoints` server-renders the row, a plain list that scrolls sideways; `PPainPointArrows` steps it from lg and `PPainPointDots` follow it below lg, both through `usePainTrack.ts`); "What changes" is four ring-icon tiles (`RingIconTile`, the video page's four glyphs); the numbers are the video page's stat cards, each share drawn as 100 dots (`PStatDots`) and rolled up from 0 (`ui/CountUpOnView.tsx`); the sources line, the phone intro over the cards and the word "kinder" are gone. Motion: `ScrollAnimator` marks `.animate-on-scroll` elements and `[data-reveal]` groups `.animate` once in view, and the page's CSS (`app/globals.css`, the `.pp-*` rules) plays from there, once: the pain-point heading rises, the cards slide up 0.12s apart (`.pp-card-in`, the row being their one trigger), each tile crosses its old habit out and pops the new line in (`.pp-change`, 0.7s apart where tiles share a row), "Ready to" arrives word by word (`.pp-w`, `.pp-wipe`; screen readers get the heading whole) and the research steps' icons pop as their rail fills (`WFoundation railMotion`, off on the video page). Under reduced motion and without JavaScript every entrance shows its end state (`tests/painPointMotion.test.tsx`). To animate anything new, follow `.claude/skills/animating-loveiq-pages/SKILL.md`.

**Entry points:**

- `ui/white/LandingPageWhite.tsx` — composition root for the video page (arm A) and the retired card page; renders each `white/W*.tsx` section in order, wrapped in `ScrollAnimator`.
- `ui/painpoint/LandingPagePainPoint.tsx` — composition root for the new page (arm B).
- `ui/white/WHero.tsx` — the hero; its right-hand slot holds question 1 (the retired card page) or `WHeroVideo` (the video page), and below lg sits right under the button.
- `ui/white-v1/LandingPageWhiteV1.tsx` — round 2's retired arm (the pre-rebuild landing).
- `ui/white/WNavSection.tsx` — white landing top nav.
- `ui/FooterSection.tsx` — site footer (reused by legal pages, glossary, trust-zone, report, about, 404).
- `ui/NavSection.tsx` — dark nav, now only used by the 404 page (`features/not-found`).
- `ui/ScrollAnimator.tsx` — IntersectionObserver-based fade-in orchestrator: `.animate-on-scroll` elements and `[data-reveal]` groups get `.animate` once in view.
- `ui/CountUpOnView.tsx` — a number that rolls up from 0 once in view (research stats only, never a price).
- `ui/RingIconTile.tsx` — the ring icon tile (flame, thermometer, message, rings).
- `ui/LandingPageTracker.tsx` — analytics pageview tracker for `/`.

**Belongs here:**

- White landing section components `white/W*.tsx` rendered by `LandingPageWhite`.
- Cross-page chrome (footer, nav) that the landing owns and other pages consume.
- Scroll/animation orchestration scoped to the landing experience.
- Tests for the above in `tests/`.

**Does NOT belong here:**

- Survey UI → `features/survey/ui/`
- Report UI → `features/report/ui/`
- Generic React primitives or brand marks → `shared/ui/branding/` (kept central — used by both landing and other surfaces).
- Anything fetching server data (landing is static content + analytics).

**Related:**

- `shared/ui/branding/LoveIQBrand` — imported by `NavSection` and `FooterSection`.
- `features/analytics/client` — `trackStartSurvey`, `trackLandingPageView`, `trackHeroVideoTap` / `Play` / `Progress` / `Complete` / `Error` / `Abandoned`.

**Conventions:**

- White sections are prefixed `W` (e.g. `WHero`, `WFAQ`); render order is the order they appear in `LandingPageWhite.tsx`.
- Animations use `animate-on-scroll` class; the `ScrollAnimator` adds `.animate` when visible.
- Mobile breakpoint for the nav: `sm=640px` (hamburger) / `lg=1024px` (full nav).
